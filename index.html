    Architecture & roadmap

    Ship on cloud LLMs.
    Then move to the edge.

    We launch on frontier multimodal models to get accuracy from the first day. Every confirmed log then becomes labelled training data, which we distil into compact models that run on the glasses and phone.

    The data flywheel

    How Phase 1 produces the training data for Phase 2.

    1. 1
      LLM auto-labels
      Frontier models label each keyframe: food, portion, activity.
    2. 2
      User-verified ground truth
      A single swipe to confirm or correct gives a verified label. Users opt in, and the data is de-identified.
    3. 3
      Distil & quantise
      A teacher model trains a compact student ViT, quantised to INT8 for Core ML and TFLite.
    4. 4
      Federated refinement
      Models keep improving on-device. Raw frames stay on the device.

    EXECUTIVE SUMMARY · SEED STAGE · 2026

    OmniVision AI: ambient life logging

    A zero-input assistant for smart glasses. It fuses egocentric vision with wearable biometrics to log nutrition, focus and physical effort automatically.

    Target Market

    • Beachhead: quantified-self users who already own a WHOOP or Apple Watch (18–40).
    • Expansion: university students, knowledge workers and athletes who care about performance.
    • B2B2C: dietitians, coaches, corporate wellness and clinical nutrition studies.

    Unique Selling Proposition

    • Zero input. Other apps need photos, barcodes or typing. OmniVision just watches.
    • Cross-modal context. Vision and biometrics together explain why the numbers changed.
    • Proprietary dataset. A labelled, first-person activity corpus that competitors can't easily reproduce.
