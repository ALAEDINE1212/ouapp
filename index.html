        Architecture & roadmap

        Ship on cloud LLMs.
        Then move to the edge.

        We launch on frontier multimodal models so the brain is accurate from the first day. Every confirmed log becomes labelled training data, which we distil into small custom models that run on the phone and the wearables.

        The data flywheel

        How Phase 1 produces the training data for Phase 2.

        1. 1
          LLM auto-labels
          Frontier models label each input: food, portion, activity.
        2. 2
          User-verified ground truth
          A single swipe to confirm or correct gives a verified label. Users opt in, and the data is de-identified.
        3. 3
          Distil & quantise
          A teacher model trains a compact student model, quantised to INT8 for Core ML and TFLite.
        4. 4
          Federated refinement
          Models keep improving on-device. Raw frames stay on the device.

        EXECUTIVE SUMMARY · UNIVERSITY BUSINESS PLAN · 2026

        LinkAI: the central brain for wearables

        An app that connects smart glasses and wearables into one AI system. It turns what they see and sense into automatic health and productivity logs.

        Target Market

        • Beachhead: quantified-self users aged 18–35 who own two or more wearables (watch or band, plus glasses).
        • Early adopters: university students and athletes who track macros, study time and training load.
        • B2B2C: sports teams, dietitians, coaches and university wellness programmes.
